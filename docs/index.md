# What is Applied Teaming?

Applied Teaming is a web app that helps students notice how they grow, as individuals and as teammates, over a semester. It quietly turns a student's own reflections into a living picture of who they are becoming. And it looks beyond the student's immediate circle: they can trace the paths of people who once sat where they sit, learn from where those alumni went and how they got there, and let that widen their own sense of what's possible.

This guide walks through **exactly what a student sees**, screen by screen, from their first sign-in to the end of a course, plus a short section on what you, as faculty, set up and can see.

## The big picture

A student's time in the app moves through three loops that reinforce each other:

1. **A living profile.** Everything a student adds (a résumé, a class, a skill) becomes a node on a personal map across six dimensions (Education, Skills, Experience, Classes, Direction, Style).
2. **A team course.** Inside a class, the student completes short **check-ins** at the start, middle, and end of the semester. Each captures the same three things: an individual goal, a team role, and how a few core teaming attributes are going. These feed three views: **Class**, **Team**, and **Personal**.
3. **Looking outward.** A student can explore where alumni like them have gone, as descriptive patterns, never as advice.

## What makes it different

- **Reflection over performance.** The point is noticing growth, not producing deliverables or hitting metrics.
- **Private by default.** A student's map is theirs. Anything communal (team reports, class views, shared stories) is opt-in and **anonymized**.
- **Invisible AI.** AI helps organize and summarize behind the scenes; the student only ever sees the result, never raw model output.
- **Safe aggregates.** Class- and faculty-level views are **k-anonymous**: only patterns shared by at least three people appear, so nothing traces back to one student.
