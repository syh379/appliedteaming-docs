# Explore alumni paths

**What it is**: the **Explore** tab. A descriptive, pressure-free look at where alumni *like this student* have gone next, answered as **patterns, never advice**.

## The landing: your possibility universe

Explore opens on **"Explore your paths"**, a composite of where alumni like the student actually went, **tailored to their own map**.

![The Explore landing.](/screenshots/explore-landing.png)
*"Explore your paths": a universe of alumni matched to the student's graph, with several ways to dig in.*

The **Your universe** panel shows each part of the map as a **ring**. Today the match reads the student's **education**, the live ring, so the alumni in view are those who share their background. Matching on experiences, classes, or direction is on the way; those rings are marked **Soon**. A count ("*122 alumni in view*") shows how many people the current match pulls in.

::: info Why education first
Education is the signal every student has on day one, so it's the one that's **live** now. As a student's map fills in, the match will read more of it, and the panel is explicit about what's driving it and what's coming.
:::

## Ways to explore

From the landing, a student can dig in several ways, plus a chat companion:

<div class="steps">

1. **Follow a thread**: walk one step at a time, what alumni from here did next.
2. **Named patterns**: a gallery of the distinct career shapes the corpus reveals.
3. **Compare two futures**: two paths side by side, timeframes, steps, endpoints.
4. **Where alumni like you landed**: an outcomes view (destinations, pay in context, and the skills those targets ask for).
5. **Open your possibility map**: the full landscape of where similar alumni went.

</div>

## Notes

- **Possibility, not prescription.** The app never tells a student what to do; it shows what others did.
- Everything here is **aggregate and k-anonymous**, patterns from groups, never a traceable individual.
- The app does not give personalized financial or investment advice.
