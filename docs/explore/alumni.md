# Explore alumni paths

**What it is** - the **Explore** tab. A descriptive, pressure-free look at where alumni *like this student* have gone next - answered as **patterns, never advice**.

## What the student sees

Four ways to explore the same alumni corpus, plus a chat companion:

<div class="steps">

1. **Map** - a landscape of where alumni with a similar background landed.
2. **Threads** - common paths from where the student is now.
3. **Patterns** - named, recurring moves ("many went from X into Y").
4. **Compare** - set two directions side by side.
5. **Chat** - ask questions of a companion grounded in that corpus.

</div>

## Notes

- **Possibility, not prescription.** The app never tells a student what to do; it shows what others did.
- Everything here is **k-anonymous** - patterns from groups, never a traceable individual.
- The app does not give personalized financial or investment advice.
