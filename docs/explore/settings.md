# Profile & settings

**What it is**: one place where a student edits their profile details and controls what's shared. Reached from the **avatar in the top-right of the header** (tap it → your profile → **Edit profile & settings**); the separate settings page has been folded in here. A left rail groups it into **Profile**, **Portfolio**, and **Account**.

## What the student can do

<div class="steps">

1. **Profile → Basic Info**: name, headline, and an "about" (school, major, grad year).
2. **Portfolio → Shared Links**: a card-based editor for social & web links (LinkedIn, website, GitHub, X, Dribbble, Medium). **Portfolio → Source**: import or manage a résumé.
3. **Account → Connected Apps**: the sign-in providers linked to the account. **Account → Data & Privacy**: a plain-language, field-by-field table of what stays private (name, contacts, employers) versus what feeds anonymized team and class insights, plus the beta terms.

</div>

> The parts of the **map itself**, education, experience, skills, and direction, are added and edited **on the map** (on Home), not in settings.

## Notes

- **Private by default.** Anything shareable starts private; the student opts in.
- When a student does choose to publish something (like a growth summary), it's **anonymized**: their name and teammates' names are stripped.
- There's nothing a student can accidentally over-share; every communal surface is opt-in.
