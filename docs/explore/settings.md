# Profile & settings

**What it is**: one place where a student edits everything about their profile and controls what's shared. Reached from **Edit profile & settings** on the Profile tab. A left rail groups it into **Profile**, **Portfolio**, and **Account**.

## What the student can do

<div class="steps">

1. **Basic Info**: name, an "about" (school, major, year), an optional résumé import, and social links (LinkedIn, personal site, GitHub).
2. **Portfolio**: edit the parts of the map by hand: **Education**, **Experience**, **Skills**, and **Career Direction**.
3. **Account**: **Connected Apps**, and **Data & Privacy** (including whether a growth summary stays private (the default) or is shared (anonymized)).

</div>

![Profile & settings, the Basic Info panel.](/screenshots/settings.png)
*One unified space: Basic Info, the Portfolio sections, and Account, each reachable from the left rail.*

## Notes

- **Private by default.** Anything shareable starts private; the student opts in.
- When a student does choose to publish something (like a growth summary), it's **anonymized**: their name and teammates' names are stripped.
- There's nothing a student can accidentally over-share; every communal surface is opt-in.
