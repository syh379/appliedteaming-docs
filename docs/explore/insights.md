# Insights

**What it is**: an honest orientation: where alumni with the student's background landed, pay in context, and the skills their target roles ask for. It's the **"Where alumni like you landed"** view, reached from the Explore landing. It **unlocks** once there's enough on the map to ground it.

## Before it unlocks

Insights stays gated until the student has two things: a **background** (a résumé or their education) *and* a **target occupation**. Until then, the page is honest about why, with the two actions that open it.

![The Insights gate.](/screenshots/insights.png)
*Until the profile is rich enough, Insights shows exactly what would open it: add a résumé, or set a target occupation.*

## Once it opens

With a background and a target set, the page fills in, all **aggregate and grounded in a real cohort**, never a promise:

- **Where alumni like you landed**: the roles students with a similar background moved into, ranked by how many people landed there (any destination shared by fewer than three is folded into a long tail to protect privacy).
- **Pay in context**: entry / median / experienced pay for the target occupations, from public wage data.
- **Skills your targets ask for**: the skills those target roles rely on, so the student can see where to grow.

## Notes

- Insights are **grounded and non-hype**: orientation, not a promise.
- They appear only once the student's profile is rich enough to be meaningful; before that, the app nudges them to add more.
- As with Explore, nothing here is personalized financial advice.
