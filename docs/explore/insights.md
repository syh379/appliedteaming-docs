# Insights

**What it is** - an honest orientation: where alumni with the student's background landed, pay in context, and the skills their targets ask for. It unlocks once there's enough on the map to ground it.

## What the student sees

![Where alumni like you went.](/screenshots/sankey.png)
*"Where alumni like you went" - from first role to now, as aggregate patterns. Any step taken by fewer than three people is folded into "Other" to protect privacy.*

- **Where alumni went** - a flow from the student's starting point through first roles to where those alumni are now; a band's thickness is how many people took that step.
- **Grounded in a real cohort** - a plain header frames it (e.g. "61 alumni · ~10 yrs experience (median) · most common now: Product").
- **Common trajectories** - the recurring ordered moves ("many went from X into Y"), plus a chat companion grounded only in that corpus.

## Notes

- Insights are **grounded and non-hype** - orientation, not a promise.
- They appear only once the student's profile is rich enough to be meaningful; before that, the app nudges them to add more.
- As with Explore, nothing here is personalized financial advice.
