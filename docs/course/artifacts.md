# Artifacts & unlocks

**What it is**: the keepsakes a course produces. A student sees the **whole roadmap from day one**, ready cards and locked cards, so they always know what's coming.

## The roadmap

Every check-in advances the record at three levels. The dashboard shows each artifact as **ready** or **locked**, with a plain "arrives after…".

![The artifact roadmap.](/screenshots/artifacts-roadmap.png)
*"Your record & artifacts", what you'll accumulate, ready → locked.*

| Level | After each check-in | When everyone finishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Individual** | **Record so far**, regenerated every check-in (your own, always live); it simply becomes your **Full record** once you've finished | **Interview stories** · **Growth summary** |
| **Team** | **Team picture so far**: a "where your team is starting" reading at the Team Charter, then how it's going, only through checkpoints everyone submitted | **Team story** (the whole arc) |
| **Class** | Overlay · Identity graph · Storyboard (live) | Persists as the cohort's shared record |

## What each keepsake is

- **Record so far / Full record**: a running snapshot of the student's goal, role, and attribute journey, updated to a new version every check-in. Once they've finished it simply becomes their **Full record**, the whole arc as one keepsake (it's the same artifact, not a separate one).
- **Interview stories**: the semester retold as STAR stories a student can bring to interviews.
- **Growth summary**: a short, first-person reflection on how they grew (theirs to keep, or share anonymized).
- **Team picture so far**: the team's roles and attribute rankings plus a short synthesis, aggregated and name-free. At the **first** checkpoint it reads as a **"where your team is starting" kickoff** (their shared goals, the attributes they each want to get right, the roles they expect to play); from the mid-point on it becomes the retrospective "how your team works".
- **Team story**: the end-of-course keepsake, a name-free narrative of the team's **whole arc**: where they started, how they worked, and how it changed by the end. Distinct from the evolving team picture above.

## Why the capstones wait

The **end-of-course keepsakes** (Interview stories, Growth summary, and the Team story) unlock only when **all teammates finish**, because they draw on the whole arc and the peer feedback that builds across the semester. The student's own **Record so far / Full record** is the exception: it's built only from their own words, so it's there from the first check-in and completes on their own finish. A student without a team unlocks everything on their own completion.

## Notes

- Team artifacts are **aggregated from the whole team's own checkpoint answers, never anyone's answer by name**. Nothing is co-signed and nothing is attributed; the app synthesizes the team's language behind the scenes and the student only ever sees the result.
- The student's **Record so far** is the one individual keepsake that updates live, since it's built only from their own words.
