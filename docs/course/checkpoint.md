# A checkpoint check-in

**What it is** - the short survey a student fills in at each checkpoint. It's the same three questions every time, so it takes only a minute or two.

## What the student does

<div class="steps">

1. From the dashboard, tap the **next-step** button (e.g., "Start: Team Charter").
2. **Your goal** - what they want to get out of the course (or how it's shifting).
3. **Your role** - the role they expect to play, or are actually playing, on the team.
4. **Core attributes** - pick up to three teaming attributes and rate each 👍 (going well) / 👉 (so-so) / 👎 (rough), with a short note on **why**.
5. At the mid and end checkpoints, add brief **peer feedback** for each teammate.
6. Save.

</div>

![The checkpoint survey - goal, role, attributes.](/screenshots/checkpoint-form.png)
*One page: goal, role, and up to three attributes with a lightweight thumb rating and a note.*

At the mid and end checkpoints, the same page adds a short **peer-feedback** section - for each teammate, what they did well and what could differ. It's shown only to the person it's about, later, in their [Personal view](/course/personal-view).

## What the student sees

The whole check-in is a single page. A microphone on every text field lets the student **speak instead of type** (transcribed on-device). The thumb ratings keep the "how's it going" part fast and light.

## Notes

- **The moment they submit**, the class view updates. Their teammates don't see their answers by name.
- **Peer feedback is shown only to the person it's about**, later, in their Personal view - never to faculty by name.
- A student can revise a check-in anytime before its deadline.
