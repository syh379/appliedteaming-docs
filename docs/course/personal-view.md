# Personal view

**What it is** - the student's own layer. Only they see it. It answers "how am *I* growing?" over the semester.

## Your growth over time

A timeline of the student's check-ins, showing how their **goal, role, and read on the attributes** have moved from start to finish.

![The personal growth timeline.](/screenshots/personal-growth.png)
*"Your growth over time" - a per-check-in timeline, plus a "how your ratings have moved" strip.*

- **How your ratings moved** - for any attribute rated more than once, the thumbs are shown in sequence (e.g., Clear team roles 👉 → 👍).
- **Each check-in** - the goal, roles, and attribute notes the student wrote at that point.

## Feedback from teammates

The peer feedback the student has **received**, shown here and nowhere else - **unattributed**, as a mirror rather than a scorecard.

![Unattributed peer feedback.](/screenshots/personal-feedback.png)
*What teammates shared - "did well" and "could differ" - never tied to a name.*

## Notes

- This layer is **private to the student**. Teammates and faculty never see it.
- Peer feedback appears here **only after everyone has finished** the relevant checkpoint, so it arrives all at once rather than trickling in.
- The student's evolving **record** (a keepsake) lives here too - see [Artifacts](/course/artifacts).
