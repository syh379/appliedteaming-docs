# Finishing the course

**What it is** - what happens when a student completes every checkpoint. The course closes out, the capstones unlock, and the semester quietly flows back into the student's profile map.

## What the student sees

<div class="steps">

1. The schedule shows **"You've completed every checkpoint."**
2. Once the **whole team** finishes, the capstones unlock all at once - Full record, Interview stories, Growth summary, and the Team story.
3. The course is **archived** - a permanent part of the student's portfolio.

</div>

![The finished course.](/screenshots/finishing.png)
*A completed course: everything unlocked, kept as a permanent record.*

## The capstones the finish produces

When the last checkpoint lands, the app generates four keepsakes automatically - from the student's own checkpoint answers, no button to press. Each opens as its own page the student can read, print, or keep.

### Full record

The whole arc, gathered as one document - every check-in's goal, role, and how each teaming attribute was going, in the student's own words.

![The full record.](/screenshots/artifact-record.png)
*Your record - the complete arc across every check-in.*

### Interview stories

The semester retold as **STAR stories** (Situation · Action · Outcome) a student can bring to interviews, each drawn from a real moment they reflected on and labeled with where it came from.

![Interview stories.](/screenshots/artifact-interview-stories.png)
*Interview stories - the student's own moments, shaped for an interview.*

### Growth summary

A short, first-person reflection on how the student grew across the semester - theirs to keep, or to share anonymized. It also becomes the "how you've grown" story on the profile map.

![The growth summary.](/screenshots/artifact-growth-summary.png)
*A ~150-word first-person growth narrative, built from the student's reflections.*

### Team story

The team-level keepsake: a plain-language **synthesis of how the team works**, drawn from the whole team's own answers about the core attributes, plus the roles they played and how they ranked those attributes. Aggregated, never anyone's answer by name.

![The team story.](/screenshots/team-synthesis.png)
*"How this team works" - synthesized from the team's own words, never attributed.*

## It feeds the map - automatically

Finishing a course updates the student's [profile map](/profile/the-map) with no button to press:

- **The course is a node** on the map (under Classes) from the day they enrolled; completion marks it done.
- **New skills** surface from what the student did and reflected on, flowing in through the same invisible signal pipeline - with compounding confidence.
- **Connections strengthen** between the course and the rest of the student's map.

## Notes

- Nothing here is manual - the map simply reflects the finished course the next time the student opens it.
- The full record and interview stories become part of a **permanent portfolio** the student keeps well beyond the semester.
