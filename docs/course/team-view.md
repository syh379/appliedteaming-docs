# Team view

**What it is** - the student's own team, seen as a shared picture. Unlike the class view, the team report is **gated**: it waits until **every teammate has submitted**.

## Before everyone's in

Until all teammates have submitted a checkpoint, the team tab shows a friendly **locked** state with live progress - a "🔒 Team report locked" panel reading *"Your team's report unlocks once every teammate submits"* and *"2 of 4 teammates have submitted the Mid-point check-in."* The class view keeps updating in the meantime.

## Once everyone's submitted

The report unlocks **through that checkpoint** - the team's role distribution and how the core attributes are trending, name-free. As the team lands each later checkpoint, the picture advances.

![The unlocked team report.](/screenshots/team-report.png)
*"Your team right now" - roles and attribute trends across the team, aggregated, no names.*

Below the counts sits a **synthesis** - a plain-language reading of how the team works, drawn from the whole team's own qualitative answers about the core attributes, plus the roles they play. It's aggregated and name-free; nothing is co-signed and nothing is attributed - the app synthesizes the team's language behind the scenes, and everyone on the team sees the same reading.

![How your team works.](/screenshots/team-synthesis-section.png)
*"How your team works" - the team's own words about each attribute, synthesized, never anyone's answer by name.*

## Why it's gated

- **No one's picture is partial.** A team report built from half the team would be misleading, so it waits for everyone.
- It keeps the team view **honest and shared** - the same picture for every teammate, only when it's complete.

## Notes

- The gate is per-checkpoint: the report shows "through" the last checkpoint the whole team finished.
- A quiet line notes what's next ("updates when everyone submits the next one - 2 of 4 in so far").
- Team artifacts follow the same rule - see [Artifacts](/course/artifacts).
