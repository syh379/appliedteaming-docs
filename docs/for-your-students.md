# Getting your students started

A one-page walkthrough you can **read aloud in the first few minutes of class**, or share with students to do on their own. It covers everything a student needs to be set up and checked in, nothing more. For the full screen-by-screen detail, see the [complete guide](/getting-started/sign-up).

::: tip The whole thing takes a student about five minutes
Create an account → a short profile setup → join your class with a code. That's it. The check-ins come later, a few times across the semester.
:::

## Before class, what to have ready

<div class="steps">

1. **The app link**: the Applied Teaming URL we set up with you.
2. **Your class join code**: a short code like `INFO213-F26`, found on your [course setup page](/faculty/setup). Write it on the board or drop it in your LMS.
3. **A few minutes of class time**: or send students this page to do beforehand.

</div>

## 1 · Create an account

Students open the link and make an account, with Google, or an email and password. First-timers tap **Create an account** at the bottom.

![The sign-in screen.](/screenshots/signin.png)
*New students tap "Create an account." No payment, IDs, or anything sensitive is ever asked, only an email to sign in.*

## 2 · Set up a profile

A short, one-time setup with a progress bar, four quick steps that seed the student's private map so it isn't empty on day one.

![Step one of onboarding, the basics.](/screenshots/onboarding-basics.png)
*Step 1: name, school, graduation year, and major.*

The four steps are: **basics** → **résumé** → **background** → **direction**. The résumé step is a simple drag-and-drop; it reads the file to pre-fill education, experience, and skills, so the map starts rich.

![The résumé step.](/screenshots/onboarding-resume.png)
*Step 2: drop in a résumé or LinkedIn PDF. Used only to build the student's own private profile.*

::: info Nothing here is graded or shared
Onboarding only builds the student's own map. Everything it finds is shown for review, and stays fully editable later.
:::

## 3 · Join your class

Once they're in, students add your course with the **join code** you shared.

![Joining a course with a code.](/screenshots/join-code.png)
*Add a course → **Join with a code** → paste the code you gave out. If you've set up teams, students land on their team automatically.*

That's the whole setup. From here the student is in your class and ready for the semester.

## What to expect across the semester

### The check-ins

A few times across the term, at the **start, midpoint, and end** (you set the dates), each student does a short **check-in**. It takes about five minutes and asks the same three things every time: their **goal**, the **role** they're actually playing on the team, and how a few **teaming attributes** are going. At the midpoint and end there's also brief, kind **peer feedback**.

![A check-in.](/screenshots/checkpoint-form.png)
*One check-in: a goal, a role, and a thumbs read on a few teaming attributes. No scores, no right answers.*

::: info Private by default
A student's reflections are theirs. What's shared with the team is **anonymized themes**, never their name. The class view is **aggregate only**. There are no scores, streaks, or leaderboards.
:::

### What they can explore

Beyond the class, students can open **Explore**, a pressure-free look at where alumni *like them* have gone next, shown as **patterns, never advice**.

![The Explore landing.](/screenshots/explore-landing.png)
*Explore: "where did people like me go next?", answered as descriptive patterns from a large alumni corpus.*

## What you'll see as faculty

You get a **faculty view** (the **Faculty** link in the top bar of your own account):

- A **cohort dashboard**: how teams and the class are doing, always **aggregated and name-free** (only patterns shared by at least three students appear).
- A **Completion** view: a simple grid of **who submitted each check-in and who didn't, by name**, for completion-based grading. It shows submission status only; the reflections themselves stay private.

See [For faculty](/faculty/overview) for the full setup and dashboard walkthrough.

## A script you can read aloud

> "Open the link I shared and make an account, Google or an email works. You'll do a two-minute setup: your name, school, and if you have a résumé handy, drop it in, it fills things in for you. Then add our course with the code on the board. That's it for today. A few times this semester I'll ask you to do a short five-minute check-in about how your team is going, it's private, there are no scores, and it's just for your own reflection and mine."
