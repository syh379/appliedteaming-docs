# Style surveys

**What it is** - two short, non-diagnostic surveys that give a student vocabulary for **how they work and learn**. They surface as concept nodes in the **Style** dimension of the map.

## The two surveys

<div class="steps">

1. **Interests** - a brief interest profiler (in the spirit of RIASEC). It describes the kinds of work a student is drawn to.
2. **Learning** - a short learning-styles survey about how a student takes things in.

</div>

![The interests survey.](/screenshots/style-interests.png)
*Short, plain-language questions - never a test.*

## What the student sees

After finishing, the results appear as **Style** nodes on the map - words like "Investigative" or a preferred learning setting - that help a student describe how they show up.

## Notes

- These are **descriptive, never a label or a score.** There's no "type" verdict.
- They're entirely optional and can be taken (or retaken) anytime.
- Style also feeds the **class identity graph** in a course - the cohort's shared work-styles, anonymized (see [Class view](/course/class-view)).
