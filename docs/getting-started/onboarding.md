# 2 · First-run onboarding

**What it is** - a short, one-time setup right after the first sign-up. It seeds the student's profile map so it isn't empty on day one. It takes a couple of minutes.

## What the student does

Four short steps, with a progress bar at the top ("Step 1 of 4"):

<div class="steps">

1. **The basics** - full name, school / program, graduation year, and major.
2. **Résumé (optional)** - a drag-and-drop importer; the app quietly reads it to pre-fill skills and experience. Skippable.
3. **Background** - a little about past experience.
4. **Direction** - where they might be headed (a target or interests), or "still exploring."

</div>

## What the student sees

Onboarding is a guided, step-by-step flow with a progress bar. Each step is short, and the résumé step is skippable - but uploading a résumé gives the profile map a rich head start.

## Notes

- **Nothing here is graded or shared.** It only builds the student's own private map.
- If a student skips the résumé, the app gently reminds them later on Home and Profile - never as a blocker.
- The moment onboarding finishes, the student lands on **Home** with a starter map already drawn.
