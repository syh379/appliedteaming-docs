# 2 · First-run onboarding

**What it is** — a short, one-time setup right after the first sign-up. It seeds the student's profile map so it isn't empty on day one. It takes a couple of minutes, and there's a progress bar the whole way ("Step 1 of 4").

## Step 1 · The basics

Full name, plus optional school / program, graduation year, and major. This becomes the first landmark on their map.

![Onboarding step 1 — the basics.](/screenshots/onboarding-basics.png)
*Step 1 of 4. Only the name is required; the rest is optional.*

## Step 2 · Add a résumé

A drag-and-drop importer (`.pdf`, `.docx`, or `.txt`). The app reads it with AI and pulls out education, experience, and skills — so the map already reflects who the student is the moment they step inside.

![Onboarding step 2 — the résumé importer.](/screenshots/onboarding-resume.png)
*Drop a résumé or a LinkedIn PDF export. Nothing is shared — it only builds the student's own private map.*

::: warning A résumé is required to finish onboarding
This step has **no skip** — a student can't move past it without a successful upload. If they have nothing to upload, a short plain-text file works. (The "optional résumé" wording elsewhere in the app is being reconciled with this.)
:::

### What happens right after the résumé is read

The app shows everything it found, grouped into **Education**, **Experience**, and **Skills**, and lets the student **edit, remove, or add** anything before it's saved. Nothing is committed until they choose **Save to profile**.

![The extracted-résumé review screen.](/screenshots/onboarding-review.png)
*"Review what we found" — the parsed résumé, fully editable. The student is always in control of what gets saved.*

## Step 3 · Background

A quick set of chips for prior roles, functions, or sectors — school projects count too. Skippable.

![Onboarding step 3 — background.](/screenshots/onboarding-background.png)
*"Where you've been" — pick what fits, or skip if just starting out.*

## Step 4 · Direction

Where they might be headed — target roles and industries — or nothing at all if they're still exploring.

![Onboarding step 4 — direction.](/screenshots/onboarding-direction.png)
*"Where you're headed" — all optional, and refinable anytime later.*

## Notes

- **Nothing here is graded or shared.** It only builds the student's own private map.
- The moment onboarding finishes, the student lands on **[Home](/profile/the-map)** with a starter map already drawn — education, experience, skills, and direction all in place.
- Everything is editable later from the profile and settings; onboarding is just a warm start.
