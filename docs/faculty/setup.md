# Claim & set up a course

**What it is**: the one-time setup that connects you to your class and gets students in.

## What you do

<div class="steps">

1. **Claim the course**: confirm you're the instructor (a claim-by-code onboarding).
2. **Add a roster**: the students' emails, optionally grouped into **teams**. When a student joins, they're matched to their team automatically.
3. **Share the join code**: hand the code (or a link) to your class.

</div>

## Notes

- **Teams are optional but recommended**: the team view and team reports only appear once students are on teams.
- The check-in schedule is per course (Info 213 uses three; the MEC ENG course uses four). Your Applied Teaming contact configures the schedule with you.
- Students who join by code are placed onto their team with no extra steps.
