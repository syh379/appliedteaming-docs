# For faculty: overview

This section covers the little you set up, and what you can, and deliberately **cannot**, see.

## Your role in three steps

<div class="steps">

1. **[Claim & set up a course](/faculty/setup)**: connect to your class, add a roster and teams, and get a join code.
2. Students **join** with that code and go through their semester (everything in the rest of this guide).
3. **[Watch the cohort](/faculty/dashboard)**: a k-anonymous dashboard of how the class is doing.

</div>

## What you see vs. what you don't

::: tip You see the shape of the class
Counts, role and attribute distributions, per-team check-in progress, and a **synthesis of each team's own words**, always **aggregated** and **k-anonymous** (nothing shown for groups smaller than three).
:::

::: warning You never see individual reflections
The app **never** shows you a student's private reflections by name, and **never** shows peer feedback attributed to a person. That boundary is enforced in the database itself, not just the interface.
:::

The one exception is **completion status**, and only status: the [Completion](/faculty/dashboard) view shows *who submitted each check-in and who didn't*, by name, so you can grade on completion. It shows a submitted-or-not flag, never the content of what was written. Everything about *what* a student reflected stays aggregate and name-free; only *whether* they submitted is shown.

This is by design: students reflect honestly because they know their individual words stay private. You get the patterns you need to support the class, and the completion facts you need to grade, without surveilling anyone.
